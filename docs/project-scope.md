# Project scope

DIGITAL MARSHAL is a student built research prototype that studies, in simulation, whether coordinating a batch of autonomous vehicles around a road incident gives lower total travel time than letting each vehicle reroute independently.

What exists in Track A: a deterministic synthetic point queue model (the preserved WP2.7 baseline), its six unit tests, its documented outputs, and provenance records.

What this is not: it is not a validated model of Dubai roads, it is not a microscopic simulation, it is not a field result, and it has not been reviewed or approved by RTA, EDGE or any other institution. It is not a deployment proposal. The WP2.7 reported audit says the same.

Out of scope for Track A: changing the model, fixing known issues, new policies, SUMO work, and the confirmatory experiment.
